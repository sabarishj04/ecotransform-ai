Methodology & Science

How EcoTransform AI Works

EcoTransform AI combines deep multimodal vision with deterministic lifecycle environmental accounting to transform domestic waste into high-value functional products.

1. Multimodal Vision Pipeline

Images undergo zero-shot visual reasoning using Google Gemini 1.5 Flash. The model evaluates resin markings, surface transparency, geometric deformation, and material density to distinguish PET (#1), HDPE (#2), aluminum alloy, corrugated paperboard, and hazardous e-waste.

2. EPA WARM Lifecycle Accounting

Avoided greenhouse gas emissions are calculated using standard emission factors from the United States Environmental Protection Agency Waste Reduction Model (WARM):

  • Aluminum: 9.10 kg CO₂e avoided per kg recycled (95% energy displacement).
  • Polymers (PET / HDPE): ~2.15 kg CO₂e avoided per kg diverted.
  • Cardboard / Paper: 1.38 kg CO₂e avoided per kg.
  • Organic Food Scraps: 0.52 kg CO₂e avoided per kg through methane avoidance.

3. Safety Guardrails for Hazardous Items

Our safety system locks out DIY transformation blueprints whenever hazardous materials (batteries, mercury tubes, chemical containers) are recognized. Users are provided safe municipal e-waste drop-off instructions to prevent physical injury and environmental contamination.
